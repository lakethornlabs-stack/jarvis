JARVIS

JARVIS is a private, self-hosted voice assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

With the owner's permission it connects to the owner's Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Tasks and Google Contacts) to read mail and events, prepare email drafts, and manage calendar entries, tasks and files on request. Sending email, inviting guests, replying to invitations and sharing files always wait for the owner's explicit approval.
